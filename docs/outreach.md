## Message

I shipped a small paid tool for agents that have to pick a US rail against a deadline. Given an amount, a deadline, and whether the receiver is a bank account or a wallet, it ranks ACH, same-day ACH, Fedwire, RTP, FedNow, and USDC on Base or Solana. RTP and FedNow stay in the answer but do not win unless nothing else can meet the deadline, because it does not check whether the bank participates. It will not screen a wallet and it will not look up a routing number.

Live API: https://settlequote.fly.dev
Repo: https://github.com/alpeshranjan/settlequote
